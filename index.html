GUIDE & RULES

📖 How to Play

1 🔍

Read the clues

Each round describes a mystery province. Higher difficulties use trickier clues.

2 📍

Click the map

Click the province or its glowing dot on the unlabelled 81-province map.

3 🌡️

Follow the heat

Wrong guesses paint the province from red (close) to blue (far) with km & compass direction.

4 🃏

Use jokers

Region 1, Extra Clue 2, Eliminate Wrong 3. Skip with P.

  • 81 provinces & borders: Click the province borders or city dots on the unlabelled map of Turkey.
  • Direction + hot/cold: A wrong click shows real km distance, compass direction, and colours the province from 🔥 red (close) to ❄️ blue (far).
  • Streak & time bonus: 3 correct in a row earns 🔥 +25 points & +5 seconds!
  • 3 jokers (−20 points each): 🗺️ Region (1) • 💡 Extra Clue (2) • ✂️ Eliminate Wrong (3)
  • Skip (P): You have 2 skips (−5 sec each).
  • 10 questions, alternating turns: The two commanders take turns, answering 5 cities each (10 questions total).
  • 60 seconds per question: Every question gives you 60 sec and up to 3 guesses.
  • Gather troops: Conquer a province in your own colour and earn +100 troops (each joker costs 20 troops; streaks add +25).
  • Jokers per player: Region (1), Extra Clue (2) and Eliminate Wrong (3) are tracked separately for each commander.
  • The Final Battle: After question 10 the armies you gathered clash on the battlefield — the biggest army wins!
